## 1. Principles

CardVerseHub handles every complaint on these principles:

* Free of charge. We never charge the person filing a complaint.
* Published criteria and a published procedure, applied identically to every account.
* The money for a disputed order stays held on the platform until a decision is reached, and goes to no one in the meantime.
* Both sides get to state their case and submit evidence before any conclusion.
* The outcome is delivered in writing, by email and by an in account notification, with reasons stated.

## 2. What we handle

We accept complaints about:

* A parcel that never arrived or was lost in transit.
* An item that does not match its description in card name, condition, language, grading details or quantity.
* A card suspected of being counterfeit or an unauthorised reprint.
* A card damaged because it was packed incorrectly.
* A seller who does not ship, or who enters an incorrect tracking number.
* Errors in the amount paid, the wallet balance, a refund or a withdrawal.
* A seller verification result you believe is wrong.
* Another user breaching the conduct rules.

We have no basis on which to resolve transactions carried out off platform, even when the two parties met through CardVerseHub. Always pay within the system so you stay protected.

## 3. Deadlines for filing

* Orders: you can file from the moment an order moves to shipping until it is recorded as complete. Where a carrier has confirmed successful delivery, the window is 72 hours from that delivery. After it closes the system records the order as complete and releases the money to the seller.
* Unboxing video: it can only be submitted while the confirmation window is still open. That is why you should record while opening the parcel.
* Wallet, refunds and withdrawals: within 30 days of the transaction.
* Content and conduct by other users: at any time.

## 4. How to file

Option 1, recommended: open the Orders page, select the order and press Report to admin. Give a reason with as much detail as possible. The order moves to disputed immediately and the money is held.

Option 2: email cardversehub.vn@gmail.com with the order number in the subject line.

Option 3: use the form on the Contact page.

Option 4: call +84 812 334 511 during working hours. A complaint made by phone still needs to be sent in writing so there is a record to work from.

Whichever route you take, keep the parcel, the box and the packing material until the case is closed.

## 5. Evidence to prepare

A complete complaint includes:

* The order number and the tracking number.
* A description of the problem and when you noticed it.
* A continuous unboxing video with no cuts, filmed from the sealed parcel through to removing the card.
* Photos of the card and the packaging showing exactly what does not match the description.
* Screenshots of the listing or of any relevant messages.
* For a suspected counterfeit: close up photos of the front, the back, the edges and any identifying features.

For sellers, the packing video uploaded at the tracking step is the single strongest piece of evidence for the condition of the card when it left your hands.

Each side may submit one video only, and it cannot be replaced once submitted, so check it before you upload.

## 6. Procedure and timelines

Step 1, receipt: the system records the complaint the moment you submit it and sends you a confirmation. The order moves to disputed.

Step 2, verification: within 24 working hours we check the tracking status and review the listing, the message history and the evidence already on file.

Step 3, the other side: the party complained about has 48 hours to respond and add evidence. No response within that period is treated as no objection.

Step 4, decision: we notify the outcome within 7 working days of receipt. A complex case needing an expert assessment or a carrier confirmation may take up to 30 days; if so we tell you why and give an expected date.

Step 5, execution: the outcome is carried out no later than the next working day after the notification.

## 7. How a case is decided

We work through the following order.

Delivery comes first:

* If tracking data shows the parcel was not delivered, the buyer is refunded. Videos do not enter into it, because proving delivery is the seller's burden.
* If there is no automated signal from the carrier, we record the status as unverified and check the carrier's own tracking page manually rather than guessing.

Once delivery is confirmed, we weigh the recorded evidence:

* Both sides have a video: the case is judged directly by the review team, comparing the two recordings in detail.
* Only the buyer has a video: the seller cannot show what was packed, so the case leans towards refunding the buyer.
* Only the seller has a video: the buyer cannot show what arrived, so the case leans towards releasing to the seller.
* Neither side has a video: the platform has no basis on which to arbitrate, and the money is released to the seller on the confirmed delivery status.

The outcome above is the system's recommendation. The final call always rests with the reviewer, who sees the whole file and may decide differently where the evidence points elsewhere.

## 8. Outcomes

The possible outcomes are:

* Full refund to the buyer: the amount is credited to the buyer's wallet, normally within the same working day. You can spend it on the platform or withdraw it to your bank.
* Release to the seller: the amount is credited to the seller's wallet.
* Partial refund, or an agreement to keep the item at a reduced price, where both sides agree.
* Return and refund: used when the item does not match its description and the seller wants it back. The party at fault pays the return shipping.

Beyond the money, and depending on severity, we may reduce a reputation score, remove listings, restrict features or close the offending account.

Shipping fees already paid to a carrier are not funds held by the platform. Whether they are refunded depends on the carrier's own policy and on which party was at fault.

## 9. Wallet, withdrawal and verification complaints

* Withdrawal rejected: the full amount is returned to your wallet with a reason. If you believe the reason is wrong, file a complaint with a screenshot of the request.
* Withdrawal delayed: every request is processed manually. If more than 3 working days pass without the money arriving, contact us with the date and amount of the request.
* Balance discrepancy: file a complaint with the date and amount. We reconcile the wallet ledger and correct any error.
* Seller verification not approved: the reason is shown on the seller page and you can submit again. If the submission was blocked because the document or bank account is already used by another account, contact us for a manual check.
* Platform fee: the 8% is charged once, at withdrawal, and is always shown before you confirm. If the amount you receive differs from the amount shown, file a complaint straight away.

## 10. Appeals and dispute resolution

If you disagree with the outcome you have 7 days from the notification to ask for a review. Say precisely what you disagree with and provide new evidence. The case is then reviewed by someone other than the person who decided it first.

If the two sides still cannot agree after the review, the dispute may be resolved through:

* Direct negotiation between the parties.
* Mediation with CardVerseHub taking part.
* A complaint to the consumer protection body or the state authority for ecommerce.
* Proceedings before a competent court in Vietnam.

Using the platform's complaint mechanism does not take away your statutory rights to complain or to bring a claim.

## 11. Bad faith complaints

Filing a false complaint, fabricating evidence, swapping a card, or abusing the protection mechanism to obtain money or goods is a serious breach.

Depending on severity we may reject the complaint, reduce the reputation score, hold the related balance, permanently close the account and refer the matter to the competent authorities. The injured party retains the right to claim compensation under the law.
