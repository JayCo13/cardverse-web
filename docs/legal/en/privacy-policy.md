## 1. Scope and data controller

This policy covers the CardVerseHub website, app and all related services, and applies to buyers, sellers and visitors alike.

The data controller is CardVerseHub, 48A Thi Muoi, Tan Chanh Hiep, District 12, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Questions about personal data can be sent to cardversehub.vn@gmail.com.

This policy does not cover third party websites or services you reach through links on our platform. Please read their own policies.

## 2. Data we collect

We collect only what a feature you actually use requires.

Account data: display name, email address, hashed password, profile photo, display language and currency.

Contact and delivery data: phone number and delivery address including province, district, ward and street detail.

Seller identity data: photographs of an identity document, a face image and liveness check data, and the full name and document number read by our verification partner. The document number is stored only as a one way hash used to detect duplicates; we do not keep it in readable form.

Financial data: bank name, account number, the account holder name returned by the banking network, and your history of deposits, purchases, sales, refunds and withdrawals. We never store your card number, card security code or banking login credentials.

Transaction data: listings, orders, offers, tracking numbers, messages exchanged within an order and the content of complaints.

Images and video: card photos you upload to list or to scan, and the packing and unboxing videos you submit as evidence.

Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, a device identifier used to count free scans, access logs and interactions with the site.

## 3. How we use data

We use data to:

* Create and manage accounts, sign you in and recover passwords.
* Display listings and process orders, payments, held funds and payouts.
* Verify seller identity and the bank account that receives money.
* Calculate shipping fees and create and track parcels.
* Recognise cards and estimate reference prices with AI.
* Receive and resolve complaints and weigh the evidence from both sides.
* Send notices about orders, the wallet, verification results and security alerts.
* Detect fraud, duplicate accounts and abuse, and protect the system.
* Improve the service through aggregated statistics.
* Meet legal obligations and valid requests from competent authorities.

We do not use your identity data, banking data or complaint content for advertising.

## 4. Legal basis and consent

We process personal data on these bases:

* Performance of our contract with you, covering purchase, payment and delivery.
* Your consent, for example when you submit documents for seller verification, upload a video as evidence or subscribe to the newsletter.
* Legal obligations, including retaining transaction records and cooperating with competent authorities.
* Our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and keeping the system safe.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out, and it may leave some features unavailable. If you withdraw consent to identity verification, for example, you can no longer sell or withdraw money.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share the minimum necessary with the following.

Other users: when you transact, the other party sees your display name, the details needed for delivery and the messages exchanged within the order.

Infrastructure and service providers:

* Supabase: database, authentication and storage.
* PayOS: payment and wallet top up processing.
* Didit: identity document verification and face checks.
* VietQR and NAPAS: bank account holder lookup.
* Giao Hang Nhanh, Viettel Post, Shopee Express: parcel delivery.
* 17TRACK: reconciliation of parcel tracking status.
* Cloudinary and Firebase: image and video storage.
* Groq and Google: image processing for AI card recognition.
* Email service providers: confirmation and alert messages.
* Netlify: website hosting and delivery.

Authorities: where there is a valid legal request, or where sharing is necessary to protect the lawful rights and property of the parties involved.

These providers may process data only under our instructions and only within the service they supply.

## 6. International data transfers

Some of the providers listed in Section 5 run their servers outside Vietnam. Your data, including card images and the data used for identity verification, may therefore be stored or processed abroad.

Where that happens we choose providers with matching security commitments, we limit the data transferred to the minimum needed, and we follow the procedures required by Vietnamese personal data protection law.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

* Keep you signed in and preserve your cart.
* Remember the language and currency you chose.
* Record a device identifier so free scans can be counted and abuse prevented.
* Measure usage so we can improve the product.

Cookies used for sign in and security are required for the service to work. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or clear stored ones, but some features will then stop working.

## 9. Retention

We keep data only as long as the stated purpose requires:

* Account data: for as long as the account remains active.
* Transaction, order and financial records: for the period required by accounting and ecommerce law.
* Identity verification results: while you remain a seller, plus a reasonable period afterwards for dispute resolution and fraud prevention.
* Packing and unboxing videos: until the order closes and the complaint window has passed.
* Technical logs: for a short period, for security and troubleshooting.

Once a retention period ends, data is deleted or converted into a form that no longer identifies an individual.

## 10. Security measures

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures:

* Encrypted transport for every connection to the platform.
* Row level access control, so a user can read only their own data.
* Every change to a wallet balance passes through controlled server code and can never be made from a browser.
* Signature verification on notifications received from the payment gateway and the verification partner.
* A limited number of staff with access to sensitive data, with access logging.

No system is perfectly secure. If an incident affects your personal data we will notify you and the competent authority as required.

## 11. Your rights

In relation to your own personal data you have the right to:

* Know what data is processed and for what purpose.
* Access it and request a copy.
* Have inaccurate data corrected. Most details can be edited yourself on the Profile page.
* Request deletion of your data or of your account.
* Withdraw consent and object to processing.

Send requests to cardversehub.vn@gmail.com with enough detail for us to confirm you own the account. We respond within 72 working hours and complete the request within 30 days at the latest.

Some data cannot be deleted immediately while you still have an order in progress, a wallet balance, or where the law requires records to be retained. In those cases we will explain why and when deletion becomes possible.

## 12. Children's data

The service is not intended for anyone under 16. Users aged 16 to 17 may use it only with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, and may not complete seller verification or withdraw money themselves.

If we discover that we have collected a child's data without valid consent, we will delete it. A parent or guardian may contact us at any time to request a check and a deletion.
