## 6. Held funds and release rules

The money you pay is not sent to the seller straight away. CardVerseHub holds it until the order ends.

Funds are released to the seller's wallet when:

* You confirm that the card arrived, or
* The carrier confirms successful delivery and 72 hours pass from that moment without you confirming or filing a complaint.

If there is no confirmed delivery signal from the carrier, the order is never released on a timer. It goes to the CardVerseHub review team for a manual decision, and the money stays held throughout.

The seller is credited 100% of the sale price. Shipping fees are not part of that payout because they were already paid to the carrier.

## 7. Shipping and tracking

The seller has 24 hours from the moment an order is paid to ship it and enter a tracking number. If that deadline passes the order is cancelled automatically, the money is refunded to the buyer's wallet and the seller's reputation is reduced. The buyer does not need to do anything.

Sellers book shipments with their own carrier account and then enter the tracking number here. The carriers currently supported are Giao Hang Nhanh, Viettel Post and Shopee Express.

Delivery status is reconciled through an independent parcel tracking service. Some carriers cannot yet be tracked automatically. For those orders the status is recorded as unverified, and closing the order is decided by our review team based on the evidence both sides provide.

We currently ship within Vietnam only.
